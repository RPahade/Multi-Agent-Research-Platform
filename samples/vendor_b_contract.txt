VENDOR B — DATA INTELLIGENCE SUITE
Master Services Agreement Extract
Contract reference: VB-MSA-2026-207

1. SCOPE

Vendor B shall provide Acme Corporation with access to the Data Intelligence Suite,
comprising analytics, reporting, and procurement benchmarking modules. This agreement
covers an initial term of two years with automatic renewal for successive one-year terms
unless terminated in accordance with Section 7.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT

The annual licence fee is USD 38,000 for up to 200 named users. Additional users are
charged at USD 220 per user per year. Vendor B does not charge an onboarding fee, but
professional services for custom integrations are billed at USD 1,400 per day. Fees are
invoiced quarterly in arrears. Vendor B reserves the right to increase fees by up to 5%
at each renewal, subject to 60 days notice.

3. INFORMATION SECURITY

Vendor B is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, with the current certificate valid until
March 2028. Vendor B has not obtained SOC 2 certification. Vendor B maintains an
information security management system audited annually by an accredited certification
body.

Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Key
management is performed internally by Vendor B; customer-supplied keys are not supported
under the standard agreement.

All customer data is stored within the European Union, specifically in data centres
located in Frankfurt, Germany and Dublin, Ireland. Vendor B does not transfer personal
data outside the EEA. This arrangement supports GDPR compliance for European operations.

Vendor B conducts penetration testing twice per year and shares full reports with
customers under NDA. Critical vulnerabilities are remediated within 30 days.

4. SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENTS

Vendor B guarantees 99.5% monthly availability. Service credits of 5% of the monthly fee
apply for each full percentage point below the guaranteed level, capped at 20% of the
monthly fee. Scheduled maintenance is capped at eight hours per month.

Support is available during business hours (08:00 to 18:00 CET, Monday to Friday), with
optional 24x7 coverage available for an additional USD 9,000 per year. Target response
time for critical incidents is four hours.

5. BREACH NOTIFICATION

Vendor B shall notify Acme Corporation of any personal data breach without undue delay
and in any event within 30 days of becoming aware of the breach, providing details
required under Article 33 of the GDPR.

6. LIABILITY

Vendor B's aggregate liability under this agreement is limited to the total fees paid in
the twelve months preceding the claim. Liability for data protection breaches is capped
at twice the annual fee.

7. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days notice to remedy. Acme may
terminate for convenience at the end of any term with 90 days notice. On termination,
data is exported in JSON format and deleted within 45 days.
