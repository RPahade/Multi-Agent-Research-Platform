VENDOR A — CLOUD ANALYTICS PLATFORM
Commercial Proposal for Acme Corporation
Document reference: VA-2026-114

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Vendor A proposes its Cloud Analytics Platform to Acme Corporation under a three-year
agreement. The platform provides data ingestion, warehousing, and dashboarding services
for enterprise procurement teams. Vendor A has operated the platform since 2017 and
currently serves 340 enterprise customers across North America and Western Europe.

2. PRICING

The annual subscription fee is USD 50,000 per year for up to 250 named users. Additional
users are charged at USD 150 per user per year. A one-time onboarding and data migration
fee of USD 12,000 applies in year one. Vendor A offers a 7% discount on the annual
subscription if payment is made annually in advance rather than quarterly. Prices are
fixed for the initial three-year term and may increase by no more than 3% annually
thereafter. There are no charges for standard support or platform upgrades.

3. DATA SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE

Vendor A holds SOC 2 Type II certification, most recently audited in November 2025 by an
independent third-party assessor. The platform is not currently ISO 27001 certified,
although Vendor A states that certification is planned for Q4 2026.

All customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3.
Encryption keys are managed through AWS Key Management Service, and customers may elect
to supply their own keys (BYOK) at no additional cost.

Customer data is hosted exclusively in United States regions (us-east-1 and us-west-2).
Vendor A does not currently offer European Union data residency. Data is replicated
across three availability zones within the selected region.

Vendor A performs annual penetration testing and provides the executive summary of the
results to customers on request. Vulnerability patching follows a 15-day remediation
window for critical findings.

4. SERVICE LEVELS

Vendor A guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime for the production environment. If uptime falls
below this threshold, service credits are issued: 10% of the monthly fee for uptime
between 99.0% and 99.9%, and 25% for uptime below 99.0%. Planned maintenance windows are
excluded from the uptime calculation and are limited to four hours per month, scheduled
outside of business hours.

Support is provided 24x7 via portal and telephone. The target response time is one hour
for critical (Severity 1) incidents and one business day for low-severity requests.

5. DATA BREACH AND INCIDENT NOTIFICATION

In the event of a confirmed data breach affecting customer data, Vendor A will notify the
customer within 72 hours of confirmation. Notification includes the nature of the breach,
categories of data affected, and remediation steps taken.

6. TERMINATION AND DATA RETURN

Either party may terminate for convenience with 90 days written notice. Upon termination,
Vendor A will make customer data available for export in CSV and Parquet formats for a
period of 30 days, after which data is permanently deleted within a further 60 days.
